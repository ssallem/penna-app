Privacy Policy — Penna

Effective date: October 18, 2026
Application: Penna (notes & memo app), package com.vravio.penna, version 1.0.0
Provider: Vravio Inc
Contact: app@binah.team

We do not collect your personal information. Penna has no user accounts, no sign-in, no server, no advertising and no tracking — every note you write stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. Vravio Inc does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Penna has no accounts, we operate no servers that receive data from the app, there is no cloud sync, and we have no ability to see the notes you create. This version sends no data off your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves what you create so it can show it back to you. This data is written only to the app's private storage on your device (a local SQLite database named notes.db and the app's local settings):

  • Note titles and body text you type
  • Checklist items and their checked / unchecked state
  • Color tags, pin markers, archive and trash status, and reminder times
  • App preferences such as theme and language
  • If you turn on the app lock, a salted SHA-256 hash of your PIN (never the PIN itself)

This data is never uploaded to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Data retention

Because Vravio Inc holds no personal information of its own, there is no data subject to statutory retention periods either.

4. Android Auto Backup

Penna allows Android's standard Auto Backup feature. If you have backup enabled in your device settings, Android may include the app's local data in your own Google account backup, so it can be restored when you set up a new device. This backup belongs to you and is controlled by your Google account settings. Vravio Inc has no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's Settings → Google → Backup.

5. Permissions and why they are requested

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — used only to show reminder notifications for the notes you created, on your device. Notification content is generated locally and is not sent anywhere. You may decline or revoke this permission at any time; the rest of the app keeps working. Reminders use a one-shot background job and the app never requests the exact-alarm permission.
  • Internet / Network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — declared because of a bundled advertising library that is disabled and never initialized in this version. The app makes no network requests of its own and sends no data over the network; the notes and settings you create never leave your device.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT) — added by the Android biometric library and used only if you turn on the optional app lock (see section 6).
  • Automatically added by libraries (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) — added to the final build by the Android job-scheduling library that runs reminder notifications. They are used only to deliver scheduled reminders on your device and are unrelated to collecting personal information.

6. App lock and biometrics

You can optionally lock the whole app with a PIN, plus your fingerprint, face or device screen lock. Biometric matching is performed entirely by Android's own secure system (BiometricPrompt): your fingerprint or face data never leaves your device and is never seen by the app or by Vravio Inc. Penna stores only a salted SHA-256 hash of your PIN, from which the PIN cannot be recovered. Note that the app lock is a privacy screen, not full-disk encryption of the notes database.

7. Advertising and tracking

This version shows no advertising. No advertising SDK is initialized, and the app does not request or use the Android Advertising ID (the AD_ID permission is removed from the app). There is no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no cookies, and no tracking of any kind. If a future version introduces advertising, this policy and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated before that version is released.

8. Sharing and sale of data

We do not share, disclose, or sell data to any third party, because we do not collect any.

9. Children's privacy

Penna is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact app@binah.team.

10. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Delete one note — remove it in the app (it moves to the trash, then clears after 30 days, or empty the trash to remove it at once).
  • Delete everything — uninstall the app; all local data is removed with it.
  • Delete without uninstalling — device Settings → Apps → Penna → Storage → Clear data.
  • Backup copies — managed through your own Google account backup settings.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export, or erase on your behalf. Requests about the app itself can be sent to app@binah.team.
